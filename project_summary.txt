# Micro-Level Project Summary & Technical Sequence: Geospatial Broomrape (Aleket) Infestation Pipeline

This document provides a highly detailed, sequential breakdown of the code blocks, operations, functions, variables, and geospatial methods executed in this Data Science and Geospatial project. The primary objective is to analyze Broomrape ("Aleket") infestation in agricultural fields using multi-spectral high-resolution satellite imagery (Planet API) and spatial ground-truth datasets.

---

## Scientific Core: The Underlying Idea & Rationale of the Project

The core scientific framework of this pipeline is built on combining remote sensing physiology (spectral signatures) and spatial structures (textural metrics) to retrospectively monitor and predict the impact of parasitic weed infestation (*Orobanche* spp., commonly known as Broomrape or **Aleket**) on host crops over time.

### 1. The Physiological Rationale (Spectral Indices)
Broomrape is an obligate root parasite lacking functional chlorophyll. It attaches directly to host roots and aggressively draws water, minerals, and carbon resources. The physiological impact manifests in the host crop *long before* the parasitic flowers visibly breach the soil.
* **NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index):** Monitors global canopy density and overall photosynthetic vigor. As the host crop's vascular system collapses due to resource extraction, NDVI declines.
* **GNDVI (Green NDVI):** Integrates the green spectral band, making it far more sensitive to subtle chlorophyll concentration changes. This acts as a proxy for early-stage leaf yellowing (chlorosis) when red bands are saturated.
* **NDRE (Normalized Difference Red Edge):** Red-edge wavelengths are highly sensitive to initial vegetation stress and canopy chlorophyll density, penetrating deeper into the crop canopy than NDVI to capture early physiological breakdown.
* **Green/Red Ratio:** Tracks chlorotic shifts directly by comparing green reflectance (healthy leaf pigmentation) to red reflectance (chlorophyll degradation).

### 2. The Textural & Structural Rationale (GLCM)
Parasitic root drain stunts plant development, causes leaf rolling, wilting, and uneven plant growth. This alters the crop's spatial geometry and canopy texture, transforming a highly structured agricultural field into a highly heterogeneous, patchy, and chaotic system.
* **Homogeneity:** Captures spatial uniformity. Healthy fields have consistent grid/row textures (high homogeneity). Localized parasitic collapse disrupts this structure (homogeneity declines).
* **Entropy:** Measures structural chaos/randomness. As the canopy breaks down unevenly, spatial randomness increases (entropy rises).
* **ASM (Angular Second Moment):** Quantifies textural order. Infected, patchy fields show high local variance, leading to a drop in ASM.

### 3. The Temporal Rationale (The 56-Day Window & Spaghetti / Spaghetti LOESS Curves)
A single satellite snapshot cannot robustly distinguish crop stress from soil variance or natural anomalies. By pulling images retrospectively over a **56-day observation window** leading to the sampling date, the pipeline constructs a dynamic timeline. Spaghetti plots overlaid with LOESS-smoothed regression curves capture the speed and rate (slopes) of physical-physiological collapse. Healthy plants maintain high, stable vigor curves, while infested cohorts display accelerated, monotonic decay trajectories.

### 4. The Spatial Rationale (Edge Proximity Buffers)
Agricultural infestations frequently spread from field boundaries, introduced by vehicles, personnel, animals, or wind. By creating metric-based inward buffers (e.g., **0.5m** inward for Edigan, **5.0m** inward for Gonen) using accurate UTM projections, the pipeline isolates **Core vs. Edge** populations. This lets us statistically determine if the border zones act as initial seedbeds or suffer from accelerated vegetative degradation due to edge effects.

## 3. Data Science & Geospatial Best Practices Enforced
* **NumPy/Pandas Vectorization First:** Avoids slow Python iterative loops by performing column-wise vectorized scaling and feature manipulation on large spatial data frames.
* **Safe Memory Management:** Utilizes Python `with rasterio.open(...) as src` context managers, ensuring that multi-gigabyte satellite orthotiles are closed immediately after pixel-level extractions.
* **Exact Projections:** Avoids manual geometric calculation errors in angular coordinates by transforming all geometries to localized UTM (EPSG:32636, Zone 36N) before calculating distance buffers or overlays.
